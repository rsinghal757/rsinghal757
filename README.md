### Hello

I'm a product designer by trade, an engineer by training, and a programmer by practice. I build software, experiments, and explanations—things that help people think, create, and get work done.

I'm interested in the entire stack of reality: from interface details and language models to computer architecture, epistemology, quantum foundations, and rockets.

### What I'm building

- **[GizzMail](https://gizzmail.com/)** — email that gets work done; a control plane for human–AI work.
- **2469 Labs** — making products on purpose, for clients we like and for ourselves when nobody's asking.
- **[The Beginning of Infinity — Hindi](https://github.com/rsinghal757/infinity-hindi)** — translating David Deutsch's book for Hindi readers.
